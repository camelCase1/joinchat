# Ever.Chat - Investor One-Pager

## The Problem
Online communities suffer from poor signal-to-noise ratio. Discord servers become chaotic with hundreds of participants, while Reddit lacks real-time interaction. Existing platforms struggle with moderation at scale and quality degradation as communities grow.

## The Solution
**Ever.Chat** is a community-owned, AI-moderated messaging platform that combines Reddit's topic-centric discovery with Discord's real-time chat. We maintain quality through small rooms (≤30 people) and lightweight reputation systems.

## Key Features
- **Topic-Based Rooms**: Focused conversations around specific subjects
- **30-Person Limit**: Prevents noise, ensures meaningful participation
- **AI Moderation**: Automated quality control and community management
- **Trust Score System**: Reputation-based user credibility (badges, account age, activity)
- **Auto-Routing**: Smart room recommendations when capacity is reached
- **Real-Time Experience**: Sub-second message delivery with presence indicators

## Market Opportunity
- **TAM**: $40B+ messaging/social platforms market (Discord $15B valuation, Reddit $10B)
- **Target**: Communities seeking higher-quality discussions (professional groups, hobbyists, educators)
- **Competition**: Discord (too noisy), Reddit (not real-time), Slack (enterprise-focused)

## Business Model
- **Freemium**: Basic rooms free, premium features for enhanced rooms
- **Community Subscriptions**: Revenue sharing with active community moderators
- **Enterprise**: Private instances for organizations and teams
- **API Access**: Developer platform for custom integrations

## Technology Stack
- **Frontend**: Next.js 15, React 19, TypeScript
- **Backend**: Express + Socket.io for real-time messaging
- **Database**: Prisma ORM with horizontal scaling capability
- **Auth**: Firebase Authentication
- **Deployment**: Modern cloud-native architecture
